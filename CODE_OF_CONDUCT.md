# Code of Conduct: By Silly Coder for Lovely Coders Everywhere

Hey friend! 👋

This project was built by a silly coder for lovely coders around the world who love building things, making mistakes, learning together, and occasionally getting paged because an AI assistant decided to reinvent the wheel at 2 AM.

We don't take ourselves too seriously, but we take keeping codebases clean, secure, and headache-free very seriously.

---

## 1. You Are Always Welcome Here

If you find something broken, something missing, or an idea that could make these guards sharper:

- **Found a false positive in a regex or test?** Open a PR. We'll merge it with joy.
- **Got an idea for a 6th guard?** Open an issue or start a discussion. Let's design it together.
- **Think our docs could be simpler or funnier?** Edit them! We love learning from everyone.
- **Brand new to open source or Git?** Never hesitate. Ask your questions without fear of judgment. We've all been beginners staring blankly at a mysterious terminal error.

---

## 2. Our Simple, Lovely Ground Rules

We don't need a 20-page corporate legal manifesto. Just three simple human rules:

1. **Be kind & generous with your patience**: Behind every GitHub username is a real human having good days and chaotic days. Treat everyone with warmth and respect.
2. **Critique the code, lift up the human**: Debate architecture and technical trade-offs passionately, but keep it constructive and friendly. "Here's why this regex might fail on Windows paths" is awesome; "Who wrote this nonsense?" is not.
3. **Stay humble, stay silly**: None of us know everything. The best engineers are curious, open-minded, ready to help others, and not afraid to laugh when a commit accidentally breaks local dev.

---

## 3. How to Contribute

1. Fork the repo.
2. Create your branch (`git checkout -b feature/my-cool-idea`).
3. Add your changes and make sure the test suites pass (`node scripts/validate-*.test.cjs`).
4. Open a Pull Request and tell us what you built!

---

*Thank you for stopping by, for caring about crafting clean code, and for making the developer community a little bit friendlier every single day.* ❤️
